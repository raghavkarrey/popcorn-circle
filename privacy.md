# Popcorn Circle privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Popcorn Circle is a movie log. You can use it alone on your phone, or join a group with a short code. There is no account, no advertising, and no tracking.

## What we collect

If you only use the app by yourself, your ratings and notes stay on your device. They are not sent to us.

If you join a group, the group server stores:

- the display name you choose
- the group code you create or join
- a random id for your device in that group
- the movies you rate while you are in the group, including the star rating and any note
- reports you submit about someone else's shared note
- the list of group members you have blocked

The server does not ask for your email, phone number, or location. It does not use cookies for ads, and it does not sell data.

Movie search can use TMDB if a catalog key is configured in the app. That request is for movie titles, posters, and descriptions. Popcorn Circle does not send your ratings to TMDB.

## How long it stays

Shared ratings stay on the group server until you delete them, leave the group, or a developer removes a note after a report.

Personal ratings stay on your device until you delete them.

## How to delete your data

In the group, tap **Leave group and delete my data**. That removes your membership, shared ratings, and shared notes from the server, and clears the group from this device.

To delete ratings that never left the phone, open **About** and tap **Delete My Ratings on this phone**.

You can also email [popcorncircleapp@gmail.com](mailto:popcorncircleapp@gmail.com) and ask for shared data to be removed.
